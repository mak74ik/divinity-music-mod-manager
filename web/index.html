How it works:

  • Simplicity: Provide standard MP3 audio files. The program converts them into engine-native Wwise Vorbis with EBU R128 loudness normalization.
  • Partial Replacement: You only need to replace the tracks you want. All empty/unmodified slots automatically keep their original game music.
  • Installation: Place the generated z_Custom_Music.pak into your game's Data folder.
  • Uninstallation: Delete z_Custom_Music.pak from your Data folder. Original game files remain completely untouched.
